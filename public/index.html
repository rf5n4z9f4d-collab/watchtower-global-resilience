How iPhone alerts work
  1. Open this live website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open WATCHTOWER from its Home Screen icon.
  4. Select your preferences and tap Enable alerts.
  5. Allow notifications, then send a test.

Push requires an always-on server, durable storage, and secure VAPID keys. It is not active until the server reports configured.

Notifications cannot be guaranteed during an internet or power outage. Keep official local alerts enabled.
